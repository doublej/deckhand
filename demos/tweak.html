rail-fade

How far should a quiet session recede?

A row fades with how long its session has been quiet. These three numbers are the whole curve, and the only way to know them is to look at the rail.

Fade floor

Fade.floor — dim, never gone. What eight hours of quiet leaves of a row.

Grace

Fade.grace — full contrast inside this, because a session that stopped a minute ago is between turns.

Rail row size

Typography.railRow — how big a session's name is set on the rail.

All three live in Sources/DeckhandKit/Design/. A page on the phone cannot write to the Mac, so whatever you land on comes back for me to apply.
